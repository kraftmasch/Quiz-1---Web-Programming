Tourist Places

Where could you spend your time in Jember?

Love Bay (Teluk Love)

Nature

Love Bay is a natural tourist attraction with a curved coastline that forms a heart-like shape when viewed from the hilltop.

Mount Argopuro

Mountain

Mount Argopuro is a complex volcanic mountain in East Java, Indonesia, with a summit reaching 3,088 meters above sea level.

Jember Town Square

City Icon

Jember Town Square is located in the heart of the city, facing the Regent's Office and the Great Mosque of Jember.

Papuma Cape

Beach

Papuma Beach is a famous tourist destination in Jember Regency, East Java, Indonesia. The name itself is an abbreviation of “Pasir Putih Malikan” (White Sand of Malikan).
